Tasik Mukena Craft

Handmade Mukena & Embroidery Artisan

Tasik Mukena Craft is a local artisan workshop from Tasikmalaya, Indonesia, specializing in handmade mukena and Muslim prayer wear products with elegant embroidery designs.


Combining traditional embroidery craftsmanship with modern Muslim fashion trends, the workshop produces comfortable, elegant, and high-quality mukena products suitable for local and international markets.


Each mukena is carefully crafted by experienced local artisans using selected fabrics and detailed embroidery techniques that reflect the authentic identity of Tasikmalaya embroidery culture.


Main Products

  • Embroidery Mukena
  • Muslim Prayer Wear
  • Premium Mukena Sets
  • Custom Mukena Orders
