About the Artisan

The makers behind Tasik Mukena Craft are local sewing and embroidery artisans who understand mukena as both a daily essential and a meaningful object used in moments of worship.

Craftsmanship

Each mukena is cut, sewn, embroidered, and checked by hand. The process balances comfort, modest elegance, and the neat finishing expected by resellers and international buyers.

Materials

The workshop uses selected prayer-wear fabrics, soft lace or embroidery accents, and thread chosen for clean detail, durability, and comfort during daily use.

Cultural Heritage

Tasikmalaya is known for Muslim fashion and embroidery craft. This mukena line carries that heritage through quiet patterns, careful finishing, and respectful design.

"A mukena should feel calm, graceful, and carefully made because it is worn in a personal moment of prayer."
Tasik Mukena Craft artisan story

Production Process

The process includes fabric selection, pattern cutting, sewing, embroidery placement, finishing, folding, and final quality review. Small-batch production allows the artisans to keep details clean while supporting flexible sourcing requests.
