Tasikmalaya Bamboo Artisan

Handwoven bamboo craftsmanship from Tasikmalaya

Bamboo Craft ID works with local artisans who shape selected bamboo into practical, warm, and naturally textured pieces using handwoven techniques rooted in West Java craft culture.

Handmade Locally Crafted Traditional Techniques Ethical Production

About the Artisan

The workshop represents small-scale makers from Tasikmalaya who continue to produce bamboo goods by hand. Their work is quiet, patient, and deeply connected to everyday Indonesian living.

Craftsmanship

Each piece is measured, split, softened, woven, and finished manually. The final form keeps the natural rhythm of the bamboo visible, giving every product subtle variations and human character.

Materials

The artisans use selected bamboo known for its light weight, strength, and organic texture. The material is chosen to support durable homeware, decor, souvenir, and lifestyle products.

Cultural Heritage

Bamboo weaving has long been part of local craft knowledge in West Java. Bamboo Craft ID presents this heritage in clean, export-ready forms for modern homes and ethical sourcing buyers.

"A bamboo product carries more than function. It carries the hand, patience, and memory of the artisan."
Bamboo Craft ID artisan story

Production Process

Production begins with material selection and bamboo preparation, followed by hand-splitting, weaving, shaping, trimming, drying, and final inspection. This slower process helps preserve the authenticity of the craft while keeping quality consistent for sourcing inquiries.
