Bamboo Craft ID

Eco-Friendly Bamboo Handicraft Workshop

Bamboo Craft ID is a local handicraft workshop based in Tasikmalaya, Indonesia, specializing in handmade bamboo products created by skilled local artisans.


Combining traditional craftsmanship with modern design, the workshop produces eco-friendly bamboo handicrafts suitable for home decoration, lifestyle products, souvenirs, and export markets.


Every product is carefully handcrafted using selected bamboo materials to maintain durability, natural aesthetics, and authentic Indonesian artisan value.


Main Products

  • Handmade Bamboo Basket
  • Home Decoration Crafts
  • Eco-Friendly Souvenirs
  • Custom Bamboo Products

Location: Tasikmalaya, West Java, Indonesia
