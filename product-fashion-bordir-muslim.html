Fashion Bordir Muslim is designed for buyers seeking modest apparel with a handmade signature: clean silhouettes, comfortable fabric, and embroidery details that reflect local craft identity.

About the Artisan

The product is made by local Muslim fashion and embroidery makers in Tasikmalaya who combine practical garment construction with decorative craft knowledge.

Craftsmanship

The making process includes pattern planning, sewing, embroidery placement, and finishing checks. Each piece is guided by hand to keep the garment neat, wearable, and expressive.

Materials

Fabric, embroidery thread, and supporting trims are selected for comfort, modest drape, and retail readiness. The materials help the apparel feel polished without losing handmade warmth.

Cultural Heritage

Embroidery is part of Tasikmalaya's visible craft language. This product translates that heritage into modern modest fashion for curated sourcing and boutique retail.

"Every embroidered line is a small signature of the place, the maker, and the patience behind the garment."
Fashion Bordir Muslim product story

Production Process

The product is developed through fabric selection, pattern cutting, sewing, embroidery detailing, pressing, and final inspection. This workflow supports small-batch sourcing with a crafted finish.
