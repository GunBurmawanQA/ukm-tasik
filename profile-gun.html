Gun’s Fashion

Gun’s Fashion is a Muslim fashion and embroidery workshop based in Tasikmalaya, Indonesia. Specializing in modest wear, mukena embroidery, and handcrafted apparel products, the business combines traditional embroidery craftsmanship with modern fashion trends.

The workshop focuses on producing quality Muslim fashion products for local customers, resellers, and potential international buyers. Each product is carefully crafted with attention to detail, comfort, and authentic Tasikmalaya embroidery identity.

Main Products:

  • Mukena Embroidery
  • Muslim Fashion Apparel
  • Modest Wear Products
  • Custom Embroidery Orders

Location: Tasikmalaya, West Java, Indonesia
