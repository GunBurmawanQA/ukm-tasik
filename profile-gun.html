Muslim Fashion and Embroidery Workshop

Modest fashion crafted with Tasikmalaya embroidery identity

Gun's Fashion is a local workshop creating Muslim fashion, mukena embroidery, and modest apparel with a handmade sensibility shaped by Tasikmalaya's textile and embroidery culture.

Handmade Locally Crafted Traditional Techniques Ethical Production

About the Artisan

Gun's Fashion is built around local makers who understand modest fashion as a blend of comfort, dignity, and decorative craft. The workshop supports customers, resellers, and sourcing buyers looking for refined Muslim apparel.

Craftsmanship

The team combines pattern making, sewing, embroidery placement, and finishing into a careful hand-guided process. Every piece is reviewed for comfort, neat stitching, and visual balance.

Materials

Materials may include modest-wear fabrics, embroidery thread, lining, and decorative accents chosen for daily comfort, elegant drape, and a clean presentation for retail or wholesale buyers.

Cultural Heritage

Tasikmalaya has a strong reputation for embroidery and Muslim fashion. Gun's Fashion carries that heritage into wearable products that feel modern while staying close to local craft roots.

"Embroidery gives modest fashion a local signature. It turns everyday apparel into something personal and crafted."
Gun's Fashion artisan story

Production Process

Production starts with fabric and design planning, followed by pattern cutting, sewing, embroidery, finishing, pressing, and quality control. The workshop can support small-batch production and custom embroidery requests for buyer inquiries.
