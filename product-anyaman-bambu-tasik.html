Handmade Bamboo Product

Handwoven bamboo goods with natural Tasikmalaya character

Anyaman Bambu Tasik is created through slow, handwoven bamboo work by local makers who transform a renewable material into warm homeware, decor, and lifestyle products for ethical sourcing buyers.

About the Artisan

This product is made by bamboo craft artisans in Tasikmalaya who rely on hand skill, eye judgement, and patience rather than mass-production shortcuts.

Craftsmanship

Bamboo strips are prepared and woven by hand, creating a textured surface that shows the maker's rhythm and gives each piece a naturally unique finish.

Materials

Selected bamboo is used for its strength, flexibility, and organic warmth. It supports lightweight products suitable for home decoration, souvenirs, and curated retail.

Cultural Heritage

Bamboo weaving reflects everyday craft knowledge from West Java, where local material and practical design have long shaped handmade household objects.

"The beauty of bamboo is honest. It keeps the trace of nature and the touch of the maker."
Anyaman Bambu Tasik product story

Production Process

The process moves from bamboo selection to splitting, weaving, shaping, edge finishing, drying, and quality inspection before packing. Each step helps keep the product authentic and sourcing-ready.
